Round two · advanced patterns · pick by number

More complex, still quiet

Eleven richer patterns, from physics, number theory and growth. They are denser and more intricate than round one, and they are meant to be read as texture, not as pictures. Same controls as before. Some take a second to compute in your browser; on the real site they would be made once at build time and saved as a small image, so visitors pay nothing.

Using this much complexity responsibly

  • One hero per page: a complex pattern as the single accent, plus the plain grid as the base. Never two complex ones in view at once.
  • Always faded from the text: the more detail, the stronger the fade, so contrast stays at AA.
  • Pre-rendered: heavy patterns become one compressed image (about 40–120 KB) made at build time. Nothing is calculated for the visitor and nothing moves.
  • Yours: number 11 is drawn from your own journey. That is the one nobody else can have.
