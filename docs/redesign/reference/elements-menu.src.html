My suggested shortlist

A, B, D, G, H, J or K, and one drawing

  1. A the floating bar with the single yellow button.
  2. B your name and live local time, replacing the status strip.
  3. D the light-serif statement with an outlined button, as the hero.
  4. J or K for the journey: the short dated list, or the ruled directory.
  5. H the small mono labels with one dot.
  6. G the single capture for the newsletter.
  7. O one line drawing, if you want a memorable detail.
  8. From Prateek Boga: S the label-left editorial layout for every section, T four doors as the homepage hub, U big figures with source links, V the centred close with a row of profiles, and R a four-word identity line if you have four words that are true.

That keeps the page to one accent (yellow), two type voices (serif and sans) and the mono label, which is the less-is-more rule.
